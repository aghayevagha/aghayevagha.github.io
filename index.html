Publications

Privacy-Preserving Shape Matching with Leveled Homomorphic Encryption (preprint)

Aghayev, A., & Imamverdiyev, Y. (2025). Cryptology ePrint Archive.

Abstract: We introduce privacy-preserving efficient 2D Fourier Shape Matching algorithm with CKKS scheme. The algorithm efficiently compares the difference of Fourier Shape Descriptors of two input shapes in encrypted domain, using SIMD operations and approximate arithmetic.

Cryptanalysis of a Post-Quantum Signature Scheme Based on Number-Theoretic Assumptions(preprint)

Aghayev, A., & Rahmani, N. E. (2025). Cryptology ePrint Archive.

Abstract: We provide cryptanalysis of a digital signature algorithm with quantum-proof claims and show a forgery attack without using signing oracle.

Implementation of Lattice Trapdoors for Quantum-Proof Identity-Based Encryption

A. Aghayev. (2025). 6th International Conference on Problems of Cybernetics and Informatics (PCI), Baku, Azerbaijan. IEEE.

Abstract: This work presents the design and implementation of plain gadget-based lattice trapdoors for quantum-proof Identity-Based Encryption (IBE), using modern trapdoor sampling techniques and efficient lattice constructions. The paper evaluates practicality, implementation factors, and security considerations for post-quantum IBE.

A Review of Cryptanalysis Techniques for Lattice Cryptography using Machine Learning

A. Aghayev, Imamverdiyev, Y., & Orujlu, O. (2025). Reliability: Theory & Applications, 20(SI 10 (88)), 176–185.

Abstract: This review surveys machine-learning-based cryptanalysis approaches targeting lattice problems like LWE and lattice reduction, highlights trends, limitations, and future research opportunities at the intersection of ML and post-quantum cryptography.

The course focuses on developing strong reasoning and problem-solving skills  (syllabus).
